# Cinematic Assets: Video Plate Prompts

These prompts are designed for high-end AI video generation (e.g., Google Veo, Midjourney + Luma, or Sora). The goal is to create seamless, soulful, Apple-like quality video plates that serve as the ambient backdrop to the WebGL scroll experience.

## Shot 1: The Valley Ascent (Arrival)
**Vibe**: The journey begins. Moody, atmospheric, moving through nature.
- **Start Frame Prompt**: `Dark, moody, cinematic shot from a car window moving through a dense pine forest in the Himalayas, thick mist rolling over the wet mountain road, dramatic dawn lighting, 8k resolution, photorealistic, Arri Alexa 65, soulful heritage vibe`
- **End Frame Prompt**: `The mist breaks slightly, revealing a majestic, soulful heritage homestay built of stone and dark wood in the Himalayas, glowing warmly in the cold blue morning, cinematic lighting, highly detailed, photorealistic`
- **Motion/Video Prompt**: `Smooth, slow-motion forward tracking shot from a car window through a dense, misty pine forest at dawn. The camera glides seamlessly as the thick mist parts, gradually revealing a glowing, warm stone heritage homestay in the distance. Cinematic, 24fps, cinematic lighting.`

## Shot 2: The Ancient Deodar Canopy (Immersive Nature)
**Vibe**: Immersive scale, looking up, ancient woods.
- **Start Frame Prompt**: `Looking straight up into a dense canopy of ancient Deodar cedars, sunlight piercing through the dark green needles in dramatic god rays, cinematic depth of field, 8k, photorealistic, moody atmosphere`
- **End Frame Prompt**: `A quiet, secluded courtyard of a Himalayan heritage homestay, weathered stone pathways, rich textures of dark wood and slate, lush green ferns, moody cinematic lighting, photorealistic`
- **Motion/Video Prompt**: `Camera looking straight up at towering Deodar cedar trees, slowly spinning and descending. Thick god rays pierce through the canopy. The camera floats down gently into a lush, dark, moody stone courtyard, revealing rich wood textures and ferns.`

## Shot 3: The Culinary Hearth (Warmth & Soul)
**Vibe**: Intimate, warm, culinary heritage.
- **Start Frame Prompt**: `Close-up cinematic macro shot of dense white steam rising off a traditional Himachali brass cooking vessel in a dark, moody kitchen, firelight glowing intensely on the textured metal, photorealistic, 8k`
- **End Frame Prompt**: `A beautifully plated, rustic heritage meal on a thick, dark wooden table, rich earthy tones, dim warm firelight, authentic Himalayan culinary experience, moody and soulful, highly detailed, photorealistic`
- **Motion/Video Prompt**: `Macro tracking shot moving through dense, swirling white steam rising from a brass cooking pot. The steam slowly clears as the camera pulls back, revealing a warmly lit, rustic wooden table with an authentic heritage meal, glowing in the firelight.`

## Shot 4: The Evening Embers (Connection)
**Vibe**: Gathering, stories, warmth against the cold night.
- **Start Frame Prompt**: `Macro cinematic shot of glowing orange embers in a rustic stone fire pit, smoke curling up into the dark night, sparks flying in slow motion, shallow depth of field, 8k, photorealistic`
- **End Frame Prompt**: `The warm, dimly lit facade of a stone and wood Himalayan homestay at night, silhouetted against the dark mountains, glowing windows radiating warmth and soul, photorealistic, moody cinematic lighting`
- **Motion/Video Prompt**: `Extreme close-up of glowing orange embers and flying sparks in slow motion. The camera slowly tilts up and pulls back through the smoke, revealing the dark silhouette of the mountains and the glowing, warm windows of a stone homestay at night.`

## Shot 5: The Cosmic Stardust (Night & Scale)
**Vibe**: Vastness, the connection to the universe.
- **Start Frame Prompt**: `A vast, breathtaking cinematic shot of the Milky Way galaxy over the dark, jagged Himalayan peaks, thousands of stars reflecting in a still, dark mountain lake, cold and majestic, 8k, photorealistic`
- **End Frame Prompt**: `Cinematic long-exposure shot of the night sky where stars are beginning to streak and blur as if the earth is rotating rapidly, dark blue and black cosmic tones, abstract but photorealistic, 8k`
- **Motion/Video Prompt**: `Time-lapse style shot starting on a still, breathtaking view of the Milky Way over dark mountains. Slowly, the earth's rotation accelerates, causing the stars to streak and blur into a rapid spin, fading to black as it transitions into the WebGL starfield.`
