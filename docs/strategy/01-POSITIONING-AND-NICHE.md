# Positioning & Niche — AI-Native Studio for AI/Dev-Tool Startups

> **Locked:** 2026-06-17 · Replaces generic SME ICP ($50K–500K ARR, EU/US) in stale docs.

---

## Positioning statement

**Turicks is The Autonomous Studio** — an AI-native creative + delivery studio for funded AI and dev-tool startups.

We design the launch experience. FounderOS — our production multi-agent OS with HITL, eval, and audit — runs the delivery. Clients get a beautiful product shipped by a studio they can trust and watch.

---

## What we are NOT

| ❌ Not this | ✅ Instead |
|------------|-----------|
| Generic SaaS development partner | AI-native studio with governed delivery |
| Design-led agency (design = equal pillar) | Design = the *finish*; AI-native delivery = headline |
| Gumroad-pack / info-product business | Premium project + retainer studio |
| Volume outbound shop | Low-volume, high-craft Proof Drops |
| 7-person team (stale BRAND copy) | Effectively solo — founder executes + learns design craft |

---

## Ideal customer profile (sharp)

**Primary:** Funded AI / dev-tool startups (Seed–Series A)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Stage** | Raised or revenue-positive; launching or re-launching |
| **Need** | Launch experience that proves they're cutting-edge (not a template landing page) |
| **Budget** | Can afford $8K+ project or $5K/mo retainer |
| **Mindset** | Understands AI agents; values governance over "vibe code" |
| **Geography** | Global remote; founder-led LinkedIn is primary channel |

**Disqualified:** Commodity website buyers, $500 starter seekers, enterprises needing 6-month RFP cycles.

---

## Messaging pillars

1. **Governed AI delivery** — HITL, eval harness, audit log. Not black-box slop.
2. **Cinematic finish** — Immersive, award-grade craft (Awwwards/Godly/Land-book target).
3. **FounderOS as proof** — Live production system, 1,098 tests, build-in-public metrics.
4. **Outcomes, not hours** — Sell the launch experience and pipeline impact, not time blocks.

---

## Elevator pitch (30 sec)

> "Turicks is an AI-native studio for dev-tool startups. We build launch experiences that prove you're cutting-edge — and we deliver them through FounderOS, our own production multi-agent OS with human approval on every external action. Beautiful design, governed delivery, fully auditable."

## Channel fit (solo operator)

1. **Referrals** — 3–5× conversion; systematize after client #1 (10% bonus)
2. **Founder-led LinkedIn / build-in-public** — inbound 14.6% vs 1.7% outbound; personal profile beats company page 5–10×
3. **Proof Drops** — 2–3 custom artifacts/week to target list
4. **Awards** — Awwwards/Godly/Land-book = cheapest credential for solo studio
