# Positioning & Niche

> **Type:** strategy · **Status:** Active (2026-06-17)

---

## Positioning Statement

Turicks is **the autonomous studio** for AI and dev-tool startups: we ship cinematic launch experiences using a production multi-agent OS (FounderOS) with human approval on every external action.

We are **not** a generic web design shop, not a vibe-coding freelancer, and not a SaaS builder competing with Lovable.

---

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Who** | Founders of AI-native or dev-tool startups (seed–Series A) |
| **Team size** | 2–20 people |
| **Geography** | Global; English-first; EU/US timezone-friendly |
| **Pain** | Launch site looks like every other AI wrapper; investors/users don't feel the product is "real" |
| **Trigger** | Funding round, product launch, rebrand, or failed agency that delivered decks not code |
| **Budget** | $8K+ for launch experience; $5K/mo for ongoing |

**Anti-ICP:** SME generic automation ($50–500K ARR, "need a technical co-founder") — deprioritized per ADR-032.

---

## Messaging Framework

### Headline (lead with Pillar A)
*"Your launch experience, shipped by an AI studio you can audit."*

### Subhead (Pillar B as finish)
*"Cinematic design. Governed delivery. Every send approved by a human."*

### Proof points (use in copy)
- FounderOS: 1,000+ tests, HITL crash-safe, eval harness
- cinematic-web: 6 presets, design systems as code
- Build-in-public: real metrics, not claims

## Competitive Frame

| Alternative | Their pitch | Our counter |

| Solo freelancer | Cheap + fast | Production OS + eval + HITL = enterprise-grade trust |

Full rules: [.claude/brand/TURICKS.md](../../.claude/brand/TURICKS.md)

## Banned Positioning

- "We do web design" (commodity)
- "Cutting-edge AI solutions" (banned phrase family)
- "$500 starter" (retired tier)
- Claiming award wins before submission
