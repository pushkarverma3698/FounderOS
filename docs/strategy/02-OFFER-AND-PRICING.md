### Tier 1 — Proof Drop (pipeline)
- **What:** Custom cinematic artifact for one named target account (hero section, micro-demo, or landing mock)
- **Price:** Free / cost of founder time (investment in pipeline)
- **Volume:** 2–3/week max — high craft, not spray-and-pray
- **Delivery:** Built via FounderOS (`marketing` copy → `engineering` `claude_code`); HITL before send
- **Goal:** 3–5 qualified conversations/month

### Tier 2 — Cinematic Launch Experience (core DFY)
- **What:** Full launch landing page + copy + deploy on cinematic-web preset
- **Price floor:** **$8,000** (value-based; scope-dependent up to $15K)
- **Includes:** Brand-aligned copy, cinematic preset build, deploy, 1 revision round
- **Trust moat:** FounderOS audit log + HITL trail available on request
- **SKU name:** `cinematic-web` Launch Experience (not "web design")

### Tier 3 — Retainer
- **Price:** **$5,000/month**
- **Includes:** Ongoing site iterations, LinkedIn content support, FounderOS-powered ops automation
- **Minimum:** 3 months

## Pricing Principles

1. **Sell outcomes**, not hours — "launch experience live in 2 weeks" not "40 hours of design"
2. **Never compete on cost** — frame ROI (funding narrative, conversion, credibility)
3. **Anchor high** — mention $8K floor in proposals; no discounting without scope reduction
4. **Hybrid OK** — fixed project fee + optional retainer upsell after delivery

---

## Scope Boundaries (Launch Experience)

**In scope:**
- Single landing page (5–7 sections)
- cinematic-web preset + customization
- Copy (marketing dept + brand validator)
- Deploy to Vercel/Netlify or client infra

**Out of scope (quote separately):**
- Full multi-page marketing site
- Auth / app backend
- Ongoing SEO / paid ads
- Cinematic Cloud SaaS self-serve

- **Retainer:** Monthly invoicing (Stripe when SCALE gate builds `payments` dept)
