## Offer ladder

### Tier 1 — Launch Experience (Project)

**$8K–$15K · 3–6 weeks**

For: AI/dev-tool startup launching or re-launching a product.

Deliverables:
- Cinematic landing / launch experience (WebGL, motion, immersive craft)
- Copy + positioning aligned to dev-tool audience
- FounderOS-audited delivery (governed, HITL on all external actions)
- Case study rights for Turicks portfolio

### Tier 2 — Studio Retainer

**$5K–$10K/mo · 3-month minimum**

For: Startups needing ongoing launch support, content, and iteration.

Deliverables:
- Monthly launch artifacts (landing updates, demo","Proof Drops" for their audience)
- Build-in-public content support
- Priority FounderOS delivery capacity
- Quarterly strategy review

### Tier 3 — Full Autonomous Build

**$15K–$50K+ · scoped**

For: Funded startups wanting a complete governed AI delivery engagement.

Deliverables:
- Full launch experience + product surface
- Custom FounderOS workflows for their ops (post-SCALE gate)
- Dedicated delivery with audit trail
- Referral program enrollment

## Pricing principles

1. **Sell outcomes, not hours** — "Launch experience that converts" not "40 hours of dev"
2. **Value-based + hybrid** — anchor on business outcome (funding signal, launch buzz, pipeline)
3. **No race to bottom** — if budget < $8K, politely decline or refer
4. **Retainer > project** — recurring revenue funds FounderOS investment
