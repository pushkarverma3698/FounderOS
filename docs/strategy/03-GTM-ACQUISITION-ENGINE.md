# GTM Acquisition Engine

> **Locked:** 2026-06-17 · Solo operator (~10h/week) · $0 paid marketing budget.

---

## Channel priority (ranked)

| Rank | Channel | Why | Cadence |
|------|---------|-----|---------|
| 1 | **Founder-led LinkedIn / build-in-public** | Inbound 14.6% vs 1.7% outbound; personal profile 5–10× company page | 3–5 posts/week |
| 2 | **Proof Drops** | Custom artifacts to target accounts — high craft, low volume | 2–3/week (Phase 3+) |
| 3 | **Referrals** | 3–5× conversion vs cold | Systematize after client #1 |
| 4 | **Awards** | Awwwards/Godly/Land-book = cheapest credential | Submit after showcases live |
| 5 | **proof.turicks.com** | Portfolio hub for all showcase work | Always-on |

---

## Build-in-public content pillars

Document these weekly on LinkedIn:

1. **FounderOS metrics** — tests green, uptime, real tool calls, HITL approvals (evidence, not claims)
2. **Showcase build progress** — WIP shots, technical decisions, design craft
3. **Governed AI thesis** — why HITL matters, anti-slop narrative, YC RFS alignment
4. **Client work** (post-first-client) — anonymized delivery stories, before/after

**Voice:** Direct, founder-to-founder. No "excited to share", "game-changer", "synergy."

---

## Proof Drops (Phase 3+)

**What:** 2–3 custom launch artifacts per week sent to the 30-account target list.

**How:**
- Built via FounderOS (research → draft → HITL → deliver)
- Personalized to each startup's product/audience
- Exceptional craft — a few *outstanding* artifacts, not volume spam

**Goal:** 3–5 qualified conversations/month.

---

## Referral engine (post client #1)

- **10% referral bonus** on closed deals
- Turn every delivery into a case study (with permission)
- Ask for intro at project close — systematize in delivery checklist

---

## Awards strategy

Submit showcase pieces to:
- **Awwwards** — Site of the Day target
- **Godly** — Curated web design gallery
- **Land-book** — Landing page showcase

Awards = social proof for cold outreach and LinkedIn credibility.

---

## Target list (Phase 0)

Build a **30-account list** of funded AI/dev-tool startups:
- Recently raised (Seed–Series A)
- Launching or re-launching product
- Active on LinkedIn (founder posts)
- Would benefit from cinematic launch experience

Optional deeper research: sub-niche within AI/dev-tool + mood board references.

---

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Avoid | Why |
|---------|-----|
| 5 Proof Drops/week (volume) | Low-volume, high-craft wins |
| Generic cold email blasts | 1.7% outbound vs 14.6% inbound |
| Gumroad-first GTM | Deprioritized — premium studio, not info products |
| Paid ads | $0 budget; founder-led channels first |

---

## Success metrics

| Metric | Target | Window |
|--------|--------|--------|
| LinkedIn cadence | 3–5 posts/week sustained | 4+ weeks |
| Showcases live | 3 on proof.turicks.com | Week 2–6 |
| Award submissions | ≥1 | After showcases |
| Qualified conversations | 3–5/month | Week 6+ |
| Closed clients | 1/month @ ≥$8K | 60–90 days |
