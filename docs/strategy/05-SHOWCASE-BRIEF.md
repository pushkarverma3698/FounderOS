# Showcase Brief — 3 Proof Pieces

> **Goal:** $10K-grade launch experiences for AI/dev-tool startups · **Host:** proof.turicks.com

---

## Why showcases (not claims)

Design craft is Pillar B — the anti-slop finish. We **show** award-grade work via self-initiated pieces. We never claim "we win awards" until we have submissions and results.

Each showcase must:
1. Look like a **funded AI/dev-tool startup launch** (not a generic agency portfolio)
2. Demonstrate **cinematic/immersive craft** (motion, WebGL, typography, sound optional)
3. Be **documented build-in-public** on LinkedIn with real FounderOS metrics
4. Be **submittable** to Awwwards/Godly/Land-book
