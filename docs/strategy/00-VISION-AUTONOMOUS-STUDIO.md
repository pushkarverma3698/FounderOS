# Vision — The Autonomous Studio

> **Type:** strategy · **Status:** Active (2026-06-17)  
> **North star for:** Turicks positioning, web design / cinematic-web service, FounderOS GTM

---

## Category

**The Autonomous Studio** — an AI-native creative + engineering studio for AI and dev-tool startups.

**One-line pitch:** *"We design the experience. Our own AI OS runs the delivery — governed, audited, and watchable."*

---

## Two Pillars

| Pillar | Role | What it is |
|--------|------|------------|
| **A — LEAD** | Headline | AI-native delivery on FounderOS: HITL, eval harness, idempotent audit, deterministic routing |
| **B — FINISH** | Anti-commoditization layer | Immersive / cinematic design craft (`cinematic-web` presets, launch experiences) — **shown via showcases, never claimed without proof** |

Design is not the category. **Governed, beautiful delivery** is.

---

## Wedge

**AI / dev-tool startups** (seed–Series A) that need a launch experience proving they are cutting-edge — and buyers who understand why ungoverned vibe-coded output is a liability.

---

## Moat Narrative

*"Beautiful product, shipped by an AI studio you can trust and watch."*

Differentiators vs v0 / Lovable / Framer / generic agencies:

1. **Trust** — every external action HITL-gated; full audit trail in Postgres
2. **Quality** — brand-validator + Claude judge (different model families) before send
3. **Taste** — cinematic-web finish layer (presets, motion, design systems as code)
4. **Proof** — FounderOS itself is the live demo; eval harness proves routing works

---

## Revenue Philosophy

- **Outcome-based**, not hourly
- **Floor:** $8K per project; $5K/mo retainer
- **Target:** 1 closed client/month = $96K–240K/yr solo — sufficient bar
- **Retired:** $500 starter tier (signals commodity; see ADR-032)

## FounderOS Role

FounderOS is **delivery infrastructure**, not the product sold to clients.

- **Now:** Route web-design work through existing `research` → `marketing` → `engineering` → `sales` departments
- **SCALE gate ($5K+ banked):** Optional `gen_asset`, MCP bridge to `cinematic-web`, `studio` department
