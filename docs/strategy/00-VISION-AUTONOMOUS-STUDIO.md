## Category

**The Autonomous Studio** — *"We design the experience. Our own AI OS runs the delivery — governed, audited, and watchable."*

Turicks is not a generic dev shop or a Gumroad-pack seller. It is an **AI-native creative + delivery studio** for funded AI/dev-tool startups that need a launch experience proving they are cutting-edge — delivered by a production multi-agent OS the founder can trust and watch.

---

## Why this exists

- Turicks is **pre-revenue, solo (~10h/week, ~$0 budget)** but owns a rare asset: **FounderOS** — production multi-agent OS (live, ~1,098 tests, HITL + eval + audit).
- **YC Spring 2026 RFS** validates the thesis: sell *finished work*, not software — software margins, agency results.
- **AI commoditizes building** (41% of code AI-generated). Value moved to what AI cannot commodify: **trust/governance, taste, distribution, outcomes.**
- **FounderOS *is* the trust/governance moat** — HITL + eval + audit as antidote to ungoverned "vibe-coded slop."
