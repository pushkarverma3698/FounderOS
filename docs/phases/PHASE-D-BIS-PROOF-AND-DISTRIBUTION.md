# Phase D-Bis — Proof & Distribution

> **Status:** 🔄 Active (started 2026-06-17)  
> **Supersedes:** [PHASE-D-REVENUE-FLYWHEEL.md](PHASE-D-REVENUE-FLYWHEEL.md) (Gumroad-first motion — archived)

---

## Goal

Land **1 premium client/month at ≥$8K** through proof (showcases + awards) and founder-led distribution — not Gumroad packs or volume outbound.

---

## Phases 0–4 (actionable)

| Phase | Window | Deliverable | Gate |
|-------|--------|-------------|------|
| **0 — Foundations** | Wk 1–2 | Strategy docs, target list, brain:sync | Docs + 30-account list |
| **1 — Proof** | Wk 2–6 | 3 showcases on proof.turicks.com | Live + ≥1 award submission |
| **2 — Distribution** | Wk 3+ | LinkedIn build-in-public (3–5/wk) | 4+ weeks sustained |
| **3 — Outreach** | Wk 6+ | Proof Drops (2–3/wk) | 3–5 qualified convos/mo |
| **4 — Close** | After #1 | Delivery + referrals + case study | 1 client @ ≥$8K |

**SCALE gate:** $5K+ banked → then build FounderOS delivery departments.

---

## What changed vs old Phase D

| Old Phase D (superseded) | Phase D-Bis (current) |
|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Gumroad packs ($29–299) | Deprioritized |
| Generic SME ICP | AI/dev-tool startups only |
| $500 starter tier | Retired — $8K floor |
| LinkedIn launch for Gumroad | Build-in-public for studio positioning |
| Cinematic-web DFY at $300K YoY businesses | Cinematic launch for funded dev-tool startups |
| FounderOS feature building | Deferred to SCALE gate |

---

## Success criteria

| Metric | Target | By when |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Showcases live | 3 | Week 6 |
| Award submissions | ≥1 | Week 6 |
| LinkedIn cadence | 3–5/wk | Week 4+ |
| Qualified conversations | 3–5/month | Week 8+ |
| Closed client | 1 @ ≥$8K | 60–90 days |

---

## FounderOS role in this phase

**Internal only** — no new departments until SCALE gate.

Use existing departments for GTM:
- **marketing** → LinkedIn post drafts (HITL)
- **research** → target list research, competitor launches
- **sales** → Proof Drop outreach drafts (HITL on send)
- **personal** → showcase file ops, deploy scripts

---

## Gate for Phase E (SaaS)

Unchanged prerequisite but **re-sequenced:**
- Phase D-Bis proves studio revenue first
- 4–6 weeks reliable FounderOS ops
- $5K+ banked from client work (SCALE gate)
- Then: SaaS pivot OR continue scaling studio — founder decides
