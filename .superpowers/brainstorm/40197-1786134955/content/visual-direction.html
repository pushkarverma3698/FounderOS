How should the spectacle encode the telemetry?

All three are real WebGPU/TSL builds on React 19 + R3F. They differ in what carries the meaning — and in how much you pay for it in daily legibility. Click one.

RECOMMENDED
Direction A
The Reactor

One dense GPGPU field — 250k–1M particles on a WebGPU compute shader. The 7 departments are attractors inside the volume. Dispatched work is particles physically streaming core → attractor.

density= token spend today
turbulence= failure rate
morph= active plan shape
heat= HITL gate pending
+ one hard idea, executed deep — what juries reward
− exact numbers need a flat HUD layer on top
Direction B
The Organism

Raymarched SDF metaballs. Each department is a soft body that grows, splits and decays with real activity; work travels as tendrils that thicken under load. Biological, not mechanical.

mass= receipts stamped
tendril= live routing
fever= error state
atrophy= idle department
+ furthest from any Jarvis dashboard on earth
− heaviest shader risk; “pretty, but what am I looking at?”
Direction C
The Console

Flat, dense, precise for daily triage — every element still GPU-rendered with shader materials — plus a deliberate 3D “dive” you open on demand for the orchestration graph.

primary= 2D instrument layer
3D= drill-down only
+ what the dashboard research actually recommends
− closest to today’s build — the thing you called 30%

My call: A. It is the only one where the crazy is the instrument rather than sitting next to it, and a thin flat HUD pinned over the volume recovers the precision that C buys by giving up the spectacle. B is the most original and the most likely to become unreadable by week two — I would rather steal its organic motion for A’s particles than build the whole surface on it.
